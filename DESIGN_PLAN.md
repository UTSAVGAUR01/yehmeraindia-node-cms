# Yeh Mera India Design Plan

## Core idea

The website should not be only an author portfolio. It should be a digital magazine with the client as founder-editor.

## Brand promise

Stories of India, by people of India.

## Main pages

- Home
- Stories
- Categories
- India Atlas
- Books
- Journalism
- Theatre
- Gallery
- About
- Contact
- Admin CMS
